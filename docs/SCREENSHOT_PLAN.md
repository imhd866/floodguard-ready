# Screenshot capture plan

Capture these after the final HTTPS deployment so the images show the real submission URL and production service worker.

## 1. Hero and trust proposition

- Viewport: desktop, approximately 1440 × 900
- State: top of page with no form selections
- Include: product name, headline, three signal levels, “Works offline,” and privacy statement
- Suggested caption: “A low-bandwidth readiness check with no account, tracking, or AI dependency.”

## 2. Observable-condition check

- Viewport: mobile, approximately 390 × 844
- State: midway through the five-question form
- Select: Heavy rain and Blocked or overflowing drainage
- Include: large radio-card targets and plain-language helper text
- Suggested caption: “Five observations a household or community volunteer can answer without specialist equipment.”

## 3. Transparent urgent action card

- Viewport: desktop, approximately 1440 × 900
- State: Heavy rain, Blocked drainage, Ankle-deep water, safe route Yes, extra help Yes
- Include: Leave safely, 8/13, all three next steps, and the calculation disclosure control
- Suggested caption: “The result exposes its score and provides a short, printable action plan.”

## 4. Deep-water safety override

- Viewport: mobile, approximately 390 × 844
- State: lowest-risk answers except Above ankle or moving water
- Expand: “How was this signal calculated?”
- Include: Leave safely at 4/13 and the override explanation
- Suggested caption: “Above-ankle or moving water triggers the highest action level regardless of the total.”

## Capture rules

- Use 100% browser zoom and hide bookmarks or unrelated tabs.
- Do not show personal information, Devpost account details, or developer tools.
- Use PNG and keep each image below the upload limit.
- Do not add fabricated device frames, user testimonials, or impact statistics.
- Add concise alt text wherever the submission platform supports it.
- Verify emergency numbers and the visible deployed URL immediately before capture.
