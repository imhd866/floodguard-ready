# Code for Humanity rules review

Reviewed 4 October 2026 against the official Devpost overview and rules pages.

## Confirmed requirements

- Participants must be students and above the legal age of majority in their country of residence.
- Companies and professional organizations are excluded.
- A public code repository link is required.
- A product demo video must be no longer than three minutes.
- All code must be written during the hackathon period; open-source libraries and frameworks are allowed.
- The project must address a real-world humanitarian or social challenge.
- Judging weights: impact 30%, innovation 25%, technical excellence 20%, user experience and accessibility 15%, scalability and sustainability 10%.

Sources:

- https://code-for-humanity.devpost.com/
- https://code-for-humanity.devpost.com/rules

## Blocking eligibility checks for the owner

Before registration or submission, personally confirm and retain evidence that:

- You are currently a student.
- You are above the legal age of majority where you reside.
- Your country is not excluded by Devpost's standard restrictions.
- Every listed teammate meets the same conditions.

## Organizer clarification required

The rules page displays “16th July 2026 - 15th January 2026,” an impossible reverse date range, while the overview displays a 15 January 2027 deadline. The overview also contains an apparently unrelated line about “self-measurement or reducing measurement-related waste.” Ask the organizer, in writing, to confirm:

1. The official hackathon start and end timestamps/time zone.
2. Whether work created after 16 July 2026 and before 15 January 2027 is eligible.
3. Whether the self-measurement/waste line is an accidental carry-over or an actual requirement.
4. Whether AI-assisted coding must be disclosed.

Do not submit until these points and personal eligibility are confirmed. This document is a practical compliance review, not legal advice.
