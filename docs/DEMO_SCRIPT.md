# Three-minute demo narrative

Target runtime: 2:45–2:55. Record at 1080p with captions and a readable browser zoom.

## 0:00–0:25 — The human problem

“When rain intensifies in Lagos, the people most at risk may have the least data, bandwidth, or time. FloodGuard Ready helps a household or community volunteer turn what they can observe into a short, calm action plan. It is decision support—not a forecast or official alert.”

Show the landing screen and the three action levels.

## 0:25–0:50 — Designed for access and trust

“It needs no account, location, API key, AI model, or personal data. After the first visit it works offline. The page uses plain language, large touch targets, keyboard controls, visible focus, high contrast, reduced-motion support, and a print-friendly result.”

Tab through the first controls. Briefly switch the browser offline if practical.

## 0:50–1:45 — Complete a realistic check

Choose: heavy rain, blocked drainage, ankle-deep water, a safe route, and someone needing extra help.

“The questions use conditions a person can observe without specialist equipment. Each answer has visible, deterministic points. This scenario returns ‘Leave safely’ because the combined concerns cross the urgent threshold.”

Submit and show the result, next actions, score, and expanded calculation.

## 1:45–2:10 — Safety override

Change the scenario to otherwise low-risk answers but choose “above ankle or moving” water.

“A safety override always triggers the highest action level for deep or moving water. The tool never encourages a user to test water depth, enter floodwater, or wait for algorithmic confirmation.”

## 2:10–2:35 — Share without surveillance

Show Copy summary and Print or save card.

“A volunteer can copy or print the action card. Nothing is uploaded. This makes the product useful on shared phones and in low-connectivity communities while minimizing privacy risk.”

## 2:35–2:55 — Credible next step

“The prototype is intentionally transparent and testable. Next, we would validate the wording and thresholds with Lagos emergency managers, disabled residents, and community organizations before any operational use. FloodGuard Ready helps people act earlier, with less uncertainty and no surveillance.”

End on the action card and repository/deployed-demo URLs.
